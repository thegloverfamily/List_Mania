ℹ️ How to Use
  1. Enter your available tiles (letters A-Z)
  2. Use ? for blank tiles that can be any letter
  3. Optionally add letters already on the board to find words that extend them
  4. Choose your preferences (minimum length, sorting)
  5. Click "Find Words" to see all possible words ranked by score

Note: Base scores shown are for tiles only. Actual game scores depend on board multipliers (Double/Triple Letter/Word scores).
